# About Me

I'm Maria Lavigina - designer, developer, Edinburgh-based, and probably thinking about plants.

---

## How I got here

I studied at Central Saint Martins. After that I worked as a designer and illustrator for years - the work I'm most proud of was a commission from the National Museum of Scotland. Five original illustrations for their permanent fashion exhibition, each two metres tall. I walked in, introduced myself to the CEO, showed my work, and got the job. First sketch - no revisions. Then came the part nobody asks about: figuring out how to take an A4 sketch and turn it into something two metres high without losing the quality.

At some point I made a deliberate pivot toward technology - convinced that creativity and coding were not opposites, and determined to find out what I could actually build. I started from scratch, working through a lifelong belief that coding required a kind of intelligence I didn't have. Three years later, that belief is completely gone.

I'm now working as a junior developer at Postcode Lottery and building full-stack projects from the ground up. PlantMate+ is what happened when the design experience and the coding ability finally ended up in the same room.

---

## About this project

PlantMate+ is the first project where I designed it and built it. Both. At the same time. That combination is what this whole journey has been about for me - and I wasn't going to cut corners on either side.

Every colour in PlantMate+ has a name. Every font was chosen for a reason. The animations aren't decorations - they're part of how the app feels to use.

The contact page is probably my favourite thing in the whole project. When you land on it, a series of illustrations builds up one after the other - each layer appearing in sequence until the final image settles into a slow breathing animation. Then, if you hover, a hidden illustration is revealed only where your cursor moves, through a CSS radial-gradient mask tracking your mouse in real time. It came from how I think about exhibitions - layered, revealing, never showing everything at once.
